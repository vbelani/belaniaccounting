Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information Belani Accounting Services Ltd. collects and how it is used. It follows British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act.

What I collect and why

The contact form asks for your name, the service you are asking about, and your message. A phone number is optional. The form opens your own email app with the message filled in. You send it to me from there, so I also receive your email address. What you type is saved in your own browser, so it is still there if you close the page, and it stays on your device. I use what you send to reply to your inquiry.

If you text me or message me on WhatsApp, your message and phone number pass through your phone company or WhatsApp, and I use them only to reply to you.

If you become a client, I collect the information needed for your tax returns, bookkeeping, or payroll. This includes your social insurance number, tax slips, bank statements, and CRA letters. I use it to do the work you have hired me for. I may also email you a reminder about a tax deadline, and you can ask me to stop at any time.

Who it is shared with

I do not sell your information. I share it with CRA and other government offices as the work requires, with the service providers I use to do the work, with anyone you ask me to send it to, and where the law requires it.

Some of the service providers I use store information outside Canada.

How it is kept

I protect your information with reasonable security arrangements, and I keep it for as long as the work or the law requires.

This website

This website does not use cookies or advertising trackers. The company that hosts this website may log visitor IP addresses and the pages requested. I do not use that information.

Questions and requests

You can ask to see the information I hold about you or ask me to correct it. Contact Varun Belani, owner, at 778-580-6174 or varun@belaniaccounting.ca.

If I cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia at oipc.bc.ca.
