Common questions

How much does it cost?

Simple personal returns start at $60 plus GST. For everything else the price depends on the work, so I give you a quote at no charge by phone or email before any work starts.

Do I have to meet you in person?

No. I can work with you by email, by phone, or in person. If you would like a visit at your home or business, we can schedule one.

What areas do you cover?

I work in Richmond, Vancouver, and the rest of Greater Vancouver.

What do I need to have ready?

You need your tax slips, receipts, last year's notice of assessment, and any letters from CRA. There is a list on each service page: personal tax, small business tax, bookkeeping, payroll, and CRA letters.

How do I send you my documents?

I will send you a secure channel for sensitive information, so please never share it by email or text. You can also drop off or mail your documents.

Will I see my return before it is filed?

Yes. I send you each return or remittance first, and I submit it only once you have signed or confirmed it.

What kinds of clients do you work with?

I work with individuals and families, and with small businesses such as contractors, tradespeople, restaurants, physicians, lawyers, and commercial businesses.

What if CRA writes to me after I file?

Call me and I will read the letter with you, tell you what CRA is asking for, and reply on your behalf. See CRA letters.

Do you need my permission to speak to CRA?

Yes. CRA needs your permission before it will speak to me, and I will show you how to give it.

Can I contact you outside tax season?

Yes. I work all year on bookkeeping, payroll, GST and PST returns, and CRA letters.

How is my information handled?

I use your information to do the work you have hired me for, and the site does not use cookies or advertising trackers. The details are in the privacy notice.

How do I start?

Text, WhatsApp, or call me at 778-580-6174, or use the form. There is no charge for the quote, and I will reply within one business day.

What happens after the quote?

If you go ahead, I send you an engagement letter that sets out the work and the price, and I start once you have signed it.
