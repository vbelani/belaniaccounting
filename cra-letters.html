Help with CRA letters in Richmond, BC

If CRA has written to you, I read the letter with you, tell you what it is asking for, and reply on your behalf.

Request a free quote Call 778-580-6174

What to do first

Many CRA letters give a date to reply by. Check the date on yours and call me as soon as you can, so there is time to put the answer together. I reply within one business day. If the date has already passed, call me anyway.

Letters I help with

  • A request for receipts or other documents to support a claim
  • A notice of assessment or reassessment that does not match your return
  • A balance owing that you do not understand or do not agree with
  • A request to file a return
  • A letter about a GST or payroll account

What I do

  • Read the letter with you and explain it in plain English.
  • Tell you what CRA is asking for and what it will take to answer.
  • Put the documents together and write the reply.
  • Deal with CRA for you by phone or online.
  • Follow up with CRA when they reply.

CRA needs your permission before it will speak to me, and I will show you how to give it.

What to have ready

I will send you a secure channel for sensitive information, so please never share it by email or text.

Price

The price depends on the letter, so I give you a quote at no charge by phone or email before any work starts.
