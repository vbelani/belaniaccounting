Returns I prepare

I prepare returns for individuals and families with employment, investment, or rental income, across Richmond, Vancouver, and the rest of Greater Vancouver.

If you are new to Canada or filing for the first time, I can prepare your first return. I also prepare returns for past years that were not filed.

What I do

  • Go through your slips, receipts, and CRA letters with you and tell you what is missing.
  • Prepare the return and explain it in plain English.
  • Send it to you for your review and signature.
  • File it with CRA once you have signed.

If CRA writes to you afterwards, I can reply for you.

Dates

Most personal returns are due April 30, and any balance owing is due the same day.

What to have ready

Please give me social insurance numbers by phone, not by text or WhatsApp.

Price

Simple returns start at $60 plus GST. I give you a free quote for your return by phone or email before any work starts. If you would like me to visit your home, tell me when you ask for your quote.
