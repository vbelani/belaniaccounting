Payroll for small businesses in Richmond, BC

I calculate each pay and its deductions, prepare the CRA remittances and the WorkSafeBC payroll report, and prepare the T4 slips at year end.

Request a free quote Call 778-580-6174

What I do

  • Calculate each pay, including the CPP, EI, and income tax deductions.
  • Prepare the remittance to CRA for each period.
  • Prepare your WorkSafeBC payroll report and calculate the premiums.
  • Prepare the T4 slips and the T4 summary at year end.
  • Prepare the record of employment when an employee leaves.
  • Reply to CRA if it writes to you about the payroll account.

Dates

T4 slips are due by the last day of February. Remittances are due on the schedule CRA has set for your account.
