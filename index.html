Financial peace of mind

My name is Varun Belani, and I have over 10 years of experience helping individuals and small businesses keep their books and taxes up to date.

Your time is valuable and taxes can cause a lot of stress. I do this work so you have more time and less to worry about, and I work around your schedule, whether your issue needs a quick call after work or a meeting at your home or business.

Over the years I have worked with contractors, tradespeople, restaurants, physicians, lawyers, and commercial businesses. I can help you lower your taxes, respond to a CRA inquiry, or see how your business is doing.

Available when you need it

I schedule calls and meetings around your hours, including after work. I keep track of your tax, GST, PST, and payroll filing dates and contact you ahead of each one, so you have time to get your documents together.

Phone, email, or in person

Most things can be done over phone or email, but when needed, I will meet you at your home or place of business, across Richmond, Vancouver, and the Greater Vancouver area.

Plain language

No accounting jargon. I will explain your personal and business finances in plain English, and you can ask me about anything on your return before you sign it.
